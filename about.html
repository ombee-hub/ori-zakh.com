About

Ori Zakh

Music producer, sound tech, composer and keyboard player.

Studied at "Rimon School Of Music" and studied under Rafi Kadishzon, an Israeli icon in the classical school of music.

Ori has produced and arranged albums and shows for Miri Mesika, Dani Robas, Shir Levi, Aya Korem, Roni Daloomi, Ohad Hitman, Tamir Hitman, Yael Nachshon, Or Taragan, Didi Shachar & Yarden Bar Kochva, Yardena Arazi, Daniel Tavori, Boaz Mauda and many many more...

Ori also participated in many musical projects as a producer/arranger and sound engineer such as Harel Skaat's 1st Album, Ofer Bashan, David D'or, "Kochav Nolad" (Israel's version of American Idol), "Avoda Ivrit" Project and more...

Music Director for the 3rd season of "Music School" (American Idol for kids & youth).

He collaborated with other composers in scoring such features as "3 Mothers" and "Aviva Ahuvati". He was the Musical director and conductor for such musicals as: "Joseph And The Amazing Technicolor Coat", "David" and "Kmo Beseret", and played Keyboards for the Musicals "Irma La Douce" and "King Salomon And Shalmai The Cobbler".

A staff member & a lecturer in "Ono Academic Music School" & in "Rimon School Of Music".
