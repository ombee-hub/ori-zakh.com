Accessibility Statement

This website is built so that everyone can use it with ease

עברית

Last updated: November 2026

Ori Zakh believes that everyone, including people with disabilities, should be able to use this website easily and comfortably. We keep working to improve the site's accessibility and welcome you to contact us about any problem or suggestion.

Accessibility level

This website has been made accessible in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA, as far as possible. The site works in all common browsers and on mobile phones and tablets.

What we have done

  • A clear page structure, with headings and HTML elements used according to their meaning, so screen readers can navigate easily
  • Full keyboard navigation: Tab moves between links, Enter activates a link and Esc closes menus and windows
  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page, and a clear outline on the element in focus
  • Alternative text for images that convey information
  • High colour contrast between text and background
  • Accessible main menu, mobile menu and photo gallery
  • In the photo gallery you can move between photos with the arrow keys and close it with Esc
  • The site is responsive and can be zoomed in the browser without content being cut off
  • If "Reduce motion" is turned on in your operating system, animations on the site are reduced automatically

The accessibility menu

Every page has a fixed accessibility button in the bottom-left corner. It opens the accessibility menu, where you can turn on the following adjustments:

  • Higher contrast (two levels)
  • Highlighted links
  • Bigger text (three levels)
  • Text spacing
  • Pause animations
  • Hide images
  • Simple, readable font
  • Big mouse cursor
  • Increased line height
  • Text alignment
  • Greyscale view
  • Reading guide

The menu can also be used with the keyboard and closed with Esc. The button shows how many settings are on, one button resets them all, and your choices are saved in your browser and applied again on your next visit.

Zooming in your browser

To zoom in, press Ctrl and + together; to zoom out, press Ctrl and -. The F11 key shows the site in full screen, and pressing it again returns to the normal view.

Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. Among others:

  • Some album covers include text inside the image. The album name and description also appear as text under each cover.
  • Photos in the gallery and on the studio page are described in general terms.
  • Videos are embedded from YouTube, and their accessibility (such as captions) depends on the platform and the original content.
  • Links to Facebook, YouTube and X lead to external services whose accessibility is not under our control.

Found a problem? We would be glad to hear about it and fix it.

Contact the accessibility coordinator

If you run into an accessibility problem or have a suggestion, please contact us and we will deal with it as soon as possible. To help us, please tell us which page the problem was on, what you were trying to do, and which browser or assistive technology you used.
