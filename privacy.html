What is this privacy policy?

Ori Zakh ("Ori") respects the privacy of the users of the website ori-zakh.com (the "Website").

This policy explains the privacy practices of the Website, including how information you provide, and information that may be collected while you use the Website, is used. In this policy, "personal information" means any information that can reasonably be used to identify you, including your name, phone number, email address and so on.

Reporting a privacy concern

If you believe your privacy has been harmed while using the Website, please contact us:

Providing personal information

Using the Website does not require registration or providing any personal information, and there are no forms on the Website. If you choose to contact Ori by email or phone, the details you provide (such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message) will be used only to respond to you.

You are not required by law to provide personal information; doing so depends on your consent and free will. When you provide personal information about a third party, you confirm that you have their consent to do so.

Information collected while you browse

The Website does not use any tracking, analytics or advertising tools (such as Google Analytics or social-network pixels). As with any website, the hosting server may record basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and time of visit, for proper operation and security only.

Social networks and external services

  • YouTube: the Shows & Media page embeds YouTube videos in "privacy-enhanced mode" (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube may collect information in accordance with its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: the fonts on the Website are loaded from Google's servers, which may receive your IP address for this purpose.
  • Social network links: the Website links to Facebook, YouTube and X. Using these services is external to and separate from the Website, and is subject to their own privacy policies.

How the information is used

Personal information you provide will be used only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry
  • To arrange and provide production, arrangement, recording and live performance services
  • To contact you when needed as part of working together
  • For the proper operation of the Website
  • To comply with any legal requirement

Sharing information with third parties

Your personal information will not be sold, rented or passed on to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • If a court order or a demand from a competent authority is received
  • In any dispute, claim, lawsuit or legal proceedings between you and Ori
  • When disclosing the information is necessary to prevent serious harm
  • At your explicit request

Where is the information stored?

The Website itself does not store personal information. Messages you send by email are kept in Ori's mailbox with the email provider, which may store information outside Israel in accordance with its own policy.

Cookies

The Website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising or statistics. It stores only technical information in your browser:

  • The accessibility settings you chose – so they are kept for your next visit
  • Your choice in the cookie notice – for the current visit only

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time through your browser settings.

Information security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information provided to us against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no measures can guarantee absolutely that information will never be exposed, and by using the Website you acknowledge these limitations.

Your right to review your information

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 1981, every person is entitled to review information about them held in a database. A person who has reviewed their information and found it incorrect, incomplete, unclear or out of date may ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Requests can be sent using the details in "Reporting a privacy concern".

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date of the latest update appears at the top of the page, and continuing to use the Website after an update means you accept the updated policy.
